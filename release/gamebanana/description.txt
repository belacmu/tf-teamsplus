TowerFall's team play only ever had two sides. This adds a third — YELLOW — and
turns the neutral column of the team-select screen into a real choice: sit there
and you're a LONE WOLF, one archer against every team at once.

It's a separate game mode, so vanilla Team Deathmatch is untouched.


WHAT YOU GET

- A four-column team screen: SOLO | BLUE | YELLOW | RED.
- A yellow team with real gold archers — body, bow and head — plus a gold aimer,
  gold dust and dash, and gold laser arrows.
- Works with custom archers: the gold and white sheets are recoloured from
  whatever art the archer has, so archer mods are handled like vanilla ones.
- Lone wolves: a white archer with white gear, hostile to everyone including
  other wolves, scoring on your own row. Nobody revives you.
- Start with any two groups: 1v1v1, 2v1v1, one team against two wolves. The only
  line-up that's refused is everyone on the same team.
- A results board with one row per group and no blank rows, and crowns, goals,
  final-kill spotlights and victory music that all follow whichever group won.
- The TEAM REVIVE variant works here too, yellow included. Wolves never get a
  reviver: a wolf answers to nobody, so nobody can bring one back.
- Third groups spawn at the point farthest from everyone already placed, so a
  three-way match doesn't start with someone in your lap.


8 PLAYERS

Install WiderSet as well and the mode goes up to 2v2v2 plus two lone wolves,
with a select screen laid out for the wide set. WiderSet is optional — without
it, everything runs at the usual four archers.


REQUIREMENTS

- FortRise 5.3.0 or newer
- TowerFall, Dark World recommended
- Optional: WiderSet, for 8 players


INSTALL

Drop the .zip into FortRise's "Mods" folder. Don't unzip it. Launch FortRise, and
"TEAMS+" is in the versus GAME MODE carousel — you need at least 3 archers
for it to be selectable.


KNOWN LIMITS

- The "even teams" treasure bonuses stay off in Teams+ matches.
- Corpses aren't recoloured; vanilla only ships blue and red corpse sheets.
- The versus intro still shows only the blue and red banners.
- A yellow revive plays the blue team's revive sound; the game ships no third one.
