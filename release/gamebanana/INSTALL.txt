TEAMS+ — for TowerFall (FortRise 5)
==================================

A third team (Yellow) for TowerFall's team play, plus LONE WOLVES: the neutral
column of the team-select screen is a real choice, and a solo archer fights
every team at once.

Vanilla Team Deathmatch is left completely alone — this is its own game mode.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
* TowerFall (Dark World recommended)
* FortRise 5.3.0 or newer      https://github.com/FortRise/FortRise
* Optional: WiderSet, for 8 players (2v2v2 + 2 wolves)
  https://github.com/FortRise/ExampleFortRiseMod/tree/main/WiderSet


INSTALL
-------
Drop TeamsPlus.zip into FortRise's "Mods" folder. No need to unzip it.
Start FortRise and the mode appears in the versus GAME MODE carousel as
"TEAMS+".


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Join 3 or 4 archers (up to 8 with WiderSet).
2. On the GAME MODE line, scroll to TEAMS+, then BEGIN.
3. The team screen now has four columns:

       SOLO  |  BLUE  |  YELLOW  |  RED

   Left/right to pick. Everyone starts in SOLO.
4. You can start as soon as two groups exist — 1v1v1, 2v1v1, a team against two
   lone wolves, whatever. The only thing you cannot do is put everyone on one
   team.
5. A lone wolf fights everyone, including other lone wolves, and scores on its
   own row.

The yellow team gets real gold archers, a gold aimer, gold dust, and gold laser
arrows. Lone wolves go white — sprite and gear — so no archer's own colours can
be mistaken for a team.

Custom archers work: the gold and white versions are recoloured from the
archer's own art when it spawns, so archer mods need nothing extra. An archer
that ships no team textures at all still becomes a proper white wolf, but stays
its own colours on the yellow team (it also cannot play blue or red in vanilla's
team modes, for the same reason).


NOTES
-----
* Levels are picked the same way free-for-all picks them, and the third group
  spawns at the point farthest from everyone else.
* "Even teams" treasure bonuses stay off in Teams+ matches.
* Corpses are not recoloured — vanilla only ships blue and red corpse sheets.


Made with FortRise. Bug reports welcome; include FortRise's log file
(FortRise/Logs) if something breaks.
